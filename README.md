AI Receptionist for a UK Plumbing Business
Turning missed calls into captured opportunities.

This project involved building an AI receptionist for a UK plumbing business to handle customer enquiries when the business owner or plumbing team is unavailable to answer the phone.

The receptionist is configured around the specific business, its services, and the types of enquiries a plumbing company receives. It can communicate with callers, understand why they are calling, collect relevant customer information, and guide the enquiry toward the next step.

What I Built
- AI Voice Receptionist that handles incoming customer calls
- Business specific AI prompt trained around the plumbing company's services, policies, and call handling requirements
- Lead capture system for collecting customer details and enquiry information
- Call handling workflow for identifying what the customer needs
- CRM integration for keeping enquiries organized
- Follow up workflow so potential customers do not get forgotten
- Receptionist dashboard for monitoring and managing incoming enquiries
- The Business Problem

Plumbers are often out on jobs when potential customers call. A missed call can mean a missed job.

Instead of sending callers to voicemail and hoping they call back, the AI receptionist gives the customer an immediate response and captures the information the business needs to follow up.

Project Objective

The goal was to create a system that works as a first point of contact for the business, helping ensure that customer enquiries are answered, captured, and moved into the sales process rather than being lost.

Built with: GoHighLevel, AI voice automation, CRM workflows, lead capture and automated follow up.
